Subject: Getting started with School Account

Hello,

Thank you for helping us test School Account: a new service that brings DfE tools, guidance and communications together in one place.

This October, we’re starting with the autumn school census. You’ll see guidance, status updates and email notifications for your return, but you’ll still submit your census through COLLECT as usual.

Sign in to School Account

You’ll need to sign in through DfE Sign-in.

What you'll see

After signing in, you’ll see a page of guidance and information about the autumn school census, including the status of your submission.

We’ll also notify you by email when the status of your submission changes.

Because we’re currently focusing on supporting the census, there’s no home page yet. We’ll add more services, opportunities and information in future, but for now we’d like to know whether this narrower set of features is useful.

To help us find out what you think, we may invite you to participate in a short research session or diary study. These are optional, and you’ll be compensated for your time.

If you don’t want to take part

Just reply to this email and we’ll remove you. This won’t affect your census submission or access to any other DfE services.
