About your status

Statuses are updated overnight. You will be emailed when the status changes.

What your status means
  • No Data: Nothing has been uploaded to COLLECT yet. You need to upload your data.
  • Loaded: Your data has been uploaded to COLLECT. You need to submit it.
  • Submitted: You have submitted your data. Your local authority or DfE will now check it.
  • Amended by Source: Your data has been changed since you submitted it. You need to submit it again.
  • Approved: Your data has been approved and is waiting for DfE to authorise it. DfE will contact you if you need to do anything else.
  • Authorised: DfE has accepted your data. You do not need to do anything else unless DfE contacts you.
